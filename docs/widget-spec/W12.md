# W12 — Chord-and-tangent addition

**Lesson:** 10. **File:** `src/widgets/W12-ChordTangent.tsx`. **Engine:** `CurveQ.slope`, `CurveQ.add`, `CurveQ.neg`, `CurveQ.integerPoints`.

## Purpose
Show one phenomenon: the line through two points of y² = x³ + ax + b meets it a third time, and reflecting
that third point gives P + Q. When P and Q are rational, everything stays rational.

## Behaviour (revised after the Phase 0 review)
- **Identity mode:** "O at infinity" (default: step 3 is a reflection) or "Choose your own O" (a third
  draggable point, drawn as a black square; step 3 joins O to P∗Q and takes the third meeting point).
  Logic in `src/widgets/lib/construct.ts`, where `null` is the point at infinity and is treated as an
  ordinary point of the curve; 9 unit tests including identity, commutativity and an associativity sample.
- **Result strip** directly under the plot shows P + Q at all times.
- **Motion:** step 1 draws the line from one edge through P then Q; step 2 runs a marker from Q to P∗Q;
  step 3 carries the point across the axis (or draws the second line). 950 ms per step, eased.
  Animation frames carry a token so a stale frame cannot overwrite a manual step change.
- **Auto-fit:** the view grows (up to 3× per axis) to contain P, Q, O, P∗Q and P + Q; it is frozen while a
  pointer is down and refits on release. Zoom −/+ multiply on top; Fit resets the zoom.
- **Hollow dots are tap targets** (30 px): tapping sends the active point (last one touched) there.

- Curves: four presets plus Custom (integer sliders a ∈ [−10, 10], b ∈ [−10, 20]).
- P and Q are dragged along the curve (pointer events with capture; 52 px hit circles; `touch-action: none`
  on handles only, so the page still scrolls when the plot is touched elsewhere).
- Hollow dots are small rational points (integer points and sums of them, thinned to at most 18). A dragged
  point within 14 px snaps to one and becomes exact.
- Keyboard: handles are focusable sliders; arrows move 18 px along the curve with a 7 px snap radius.
- Steps 0–3 with Back / Next / Replay. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing animates: Replay resets to
  step 0 and the learner advances by hand.
- Readout: exact fractions when both points are exact; otherwise decimals marked ≈. Integers longer than 14
  digits show the first 6 and last 4 with a note giving the digit counts.

## Edge cases (all tested)
| Case | Behaviour | Test |
|---|---|---|
| P = Q | tangent line, dashed; label 2P | e2e "tangent"; engine "add(P,P)" |
| x equal, y opposite | vertical line, O marker, readout "O" | engine "vertical lines give O" |
| P = Q with y = 0 | vertical tangent, 2P = O | e2e on y² = x³ − x |
| 4a³ + 27b² = 0 | warning, construction off | e2e "singular"; engine `SingularCurveError` |
| Huge numerators | clipped display, no layout overflow at 390 px | e2e "repeated adding" |
| Result far away | view grows up to 3×, then a note says the picture is clamped | manual |
| Own O: P∗Q at infinity | line OT is the vertical through O | unit |
| Own O: P + Q at infinity | marker and text "is at infinity" (it is not the identity in this mode) | unit |
| Own O: P∗Q = O | tangent at O | unit (identity test) |
| Q dragged onto O | P + Q = P | e2e |

## Known limits
- Free (non-rational) points use floating point, so their readout is approximate by design.
- Only the short form y² = x³ + ax + b; the x² term arrives with Lesson 12.
